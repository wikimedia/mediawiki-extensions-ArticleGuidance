<instructions>
Articles about actors are frequently deleted unless the person has received significant coverage in reliable, independent sources. Appearing in a production, having an agent, or being credited in a film or series is not enough on its own. The person needs in-depth coverage from sources that are not connected to them or their work.
Quick considerations:
* Focus on the person's career and documented achievements, not their personal life.
* Cite independent journalism and reviews, not the actor's own website, social media, or press releases from their management.
* Do not write about yourself, your family, or your friends.
* If the person is known primarily by a stage name, use that name as the article title and note their legal name in the introduction.
</instructions>
