<instructions>
Not all albums need their own Wikipedia article. An album may be notable enough for a separate article when it has received significant coverage in reliable, independent music publications, charted widely, won major awards, or represents a significant moment in an artist's career or music history. Albums by notable artists are often considered suitable, but each should be assessed based on available independent coverage.

Quick considerations:
* Make sure articles already exist for the artist and the record label before creating an album article.
* Do not reproduce song lyrics. Focus on the album's context, reception, and significance.
* Do not write about an album you, your family, or your friends created.
* Album titles should be in italics.
</instructions>
