<instructions>
Articles about animal species are appropriate when the species has been formally described in scientific literature and has a recognised scientific name. Any animal species that has been given a formal scientific name in a peer-reviewed publication is considered appropriate for a Wikipedia article. The article should accurately represent current scientific understanding based on peer-reviewed sources.

Quick considerations:
* Use the species' common name as the article title where one exists, and include the scientific name in the lead.
* Species names (common names) are not capitalised unless they contain a proper noun.
* Base all scientific claims on peer-reviewed publications. Do not speculate beyond what sources say.
* Conservation status should follow the IUCN Red List, which is the preferred source.
</instructions>
