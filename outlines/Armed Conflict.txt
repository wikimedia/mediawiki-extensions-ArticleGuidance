<instructions>
Articles about armed conflicts (wars, battles, sieges, insurgencies, and other organised violent confrontations) are appropriate when the conflict has received significant coverage in reliable, independent sources. The article must present all sides of the conflict neutrally, based on verifiable facts from independent sources, not on the claims of any party to the conflict.
Quick considerations:
* Write from a strictly neutral perspective. Do not take sides, use loaded language, or present one party's framing of events as fact.
* Cite multiple independent sources representing different perspectives where possible. Rely on established newswires, academic publications, and recognised international organisations.
* Avoid primary sources produced by parties to the conflict (military communiqués, government statements, partisan press) unless attributed clearly and balanced against other sources.
* Casualty figures and territorial claims are often contested. Present all credible estimates with attribution.
* Do not describe an ongoing conflict in real time. Wikipedia is a reference work, not a news service.
</instructions>
