<instructions>
Astronomical objects (stars, planets, galaxies, nebulae, asteroids, comets, and other objects observed beyond Earth's atmosphere) are generally appropriate subjects for Wikipedia articles when they are catalogued in an authoritative astronomical database and have been the subject of peer-reviewed scientific publications.
Quick considerations:
* Use the object's standard catalogue designation as the article title (e.g. NGC 224, HD 40307). If the object has a widely used common name, use that and include the catalogue designation in the lead.
* Base all physical and scientific claims on peer-reviewed publications or authoritative astronomical databases.
* Do not include speculative content about extraterrestrial life or unverified discoveries.
* Cite the discovery paper and catalogue entry for basic facts. Do not rely on news articles or popular science magazines as the sole source.
* For Solar System objects, follow Wikipedia:WikiProject Solar System guidelines.
</instructions>
