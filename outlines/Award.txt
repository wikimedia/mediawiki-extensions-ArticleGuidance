<article-guidance article-type="Q618779" notability-risk="sources">
<instructions>
Articles about awards are appropriate when the award has received significant coverage in reliable, independent sources. An award being given regularly, or being awarded by a notable organisation, does not by itself establish notability. Coverage must be substantive and address the award itself, not just list its recipients.
Quick considerations:
* Focus on what the award is for, who gives it, and why it is significant - not solely on listing recipients.
* Cite independent sources that cover the award itself, not just press releases from the awarding body.
* Do not write about an award given by an organisation you are affiliated with.
* Avoid making the article a directory of past winners. If the list is long, it belongs in a separate list article.
* If another article exists for the award name, adjust the title to include the awarding organisation or field in parentheses.
</instructions>
