<instructions>
Articles about buildings (offices, factories, theatres, stadiums, and other structures) are appropriate when the building has received significant coverage in reliable, independent sources, or has recognised historical, architectural, or cultural significance. Ordinary residential buildings, recently constructed buildings without independent coverage, and buildings primarily notable only to their owners or occupants typically do not warrant their own article.

Quick considerations:
* Establish why this specific building is notable. Its age, architectural significance, historical events, or cultural importance, using independent sources.
* Distinguish between the building as a physical structure and any organisation or business that occupies it. These may need separate articles.
* Base architectural and historical claims on independent scholarly or journalistic sources, not solely on the building owner's or developer's materials.
* If the building is listed on an official heritage register, mention this clearly and cite the relevant register entry.
* Do not write about a building you own, manage, or have a personal or professional connection to.

</instructions>
