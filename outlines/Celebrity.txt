<instructions>
Articles about socialites and public figures known primarily for their social prominence are among the most frequently deleted on Wikipedia. Fame, social media following, or regular appearance at public events is not enough. The person needs significant, independent coverage in reliable sources that goes beyond gossip columns, event listings, or promotional profiles.
Quick considerations:
*Coverage must be substantive and independent, not interviews, press releases, or publicity.
*Write about the person's public role and any documented activities or contributions. Avoid focusing on personal life unless it is well-sourced and significant for general reference.
*Do not use gossip sites, tabloids, or social media as sources.
*Do not write about yourself, your family, or your friends.
</instructions>
