<instructions>
Chemical elements are generally considered notable if they appear in the periodic table and are recognised by the International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry (IUPAC). Articles should be based on significant coverage in peer-reviewed scientific literature.
Quick considerations:
*Use the IUPAC-approved name and symbol. Element names are common nouns, do not capitalise them when written as words.
*Include the element's symbol, atomic number, and position in the periodic table in the lead.
*Do not include unsourced claims about biological effects, toxicity, or applications.
<instructions>
