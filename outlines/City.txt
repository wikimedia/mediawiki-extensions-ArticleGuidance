<instructions>
Cities and large urban settlements with recognised administrative status are considered notable and do not require additional sourcing to create an article. The article should accurately represent the city as described in reliable, independent sources.

Quick considerations:
* State the city's location, country, and population clearly in the lead.
* Use official national statistics and census data for population and demographic figures.
* Avoid promotional language. Describe the city factually based on what independent sources say.
* Do not describe the city as 'one of the most important/interesting/beautiful' unless directly supported by a cited independent source.
</instructions>
