<instructions>
Articles about businesses are among the most frequently deleted on Wikipedia. A company needs significant, independent coverage in reliable sources — not press releases, not its own website, and not directory listings. Coverage must be substantive, not just passing mentions.

Quick considerations:

* Write about what the company does and why it is notable, using independent sources.
* Avoid copying from the company's own website or press materials — this reads as promotional and will be flagged for deletion.
* Focus on what independent journalists and analysts have written about the company.
* Don’t write about companies where you, your family, or your friends participate.
</instructions>

<recommended-sources>
  <info>Major business and financial press with editorial standards (e.g. Financial Times, Reuters, Bloomberg, The Economist), </info>
  <info>Official regulatory filings and government records (e.g. Companies House, SEC filings), </info>
  <info>Peer-reviewed business or industry academic publications</info>
  <info>Established industry trade press with editorial oversight</info>
</recommended-sources>

<discouraged-sources>
  <info>Company websites, press releases and investor relations materials</info>
  <info>PR and press release distribution services</info>
  <info>Social media accounts and promotional content</info>
  <info>Directory listings and aggregator databases used as the sole source</info>
</discouraged-sources>

</article-guidance>
