<instructions>
Sovereign countries recognised by the United Nations or by a significant number of UN member states are considered notable and do not require additional sourcing to create an article. The article should accurately represent the country as described in reliable, independent sources.
Quick considerations:
* Write from a neutral perspective. Avoid language that takes sides in territorial disputes or political controversies.
* Use the country's official name in the lead, followed by the commonly used name where they differ.
* Demographic and economic data should be sourced from official national statistics, the UN, or the World Bank.
* Do not use the article to advocate for any political position about the country's government or status.
</instructions>
