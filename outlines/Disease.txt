<instructions>
Articles about diseases are only appropriate when the condition is recognised by mainstream medical sources. Self-diagnosed conditions, informal illness categories, and conditions described only in non-peer-reviewed sources are not suitable for Wikipedia articles.

Quick considerations:

*Write from the perspective of established medical and scientific consensus. Avoid fringe theories or unverified treatments.

*Use medical terminology correctly and link technical terms on first use.

*Do not write in the second person ("you may experience..."). Wikipedia is an encyclopaedia, not a patient information leaflet.

*Cite peer-reviewed publications, clinical guidelines and recognised medical organisations, not news articles or personal accounts.
</instructions>
