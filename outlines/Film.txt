<instructions>
Wikipedia editors cover films only when there is enough evidence of them being notable.
Films are often considered notable when they are widely distributed, have full-length reviews from independent publications, and received major awards or represent a unique accomplishment in cinema. 

Quick considerations:

* If another article exists for the film title, adjust the title to include “(year film)” at the end. For example: “Titanic (1997 film)”.
* Avoid information from promotional material or press releases by those participating in the production or distribution of the film. This information often has a risk of not being neutral.
* Don’t write about a film where you, or someone you know, participated in.
</instructions>
