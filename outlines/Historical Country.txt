<instructions>
Articles about historical countries, states, or territories that no longer exist are appropriate when they have received significant coverage in reliable, independent scholarly sources. The article should accurately represent the entity as documented in historical sources, not through speculation or original research.
Quick considerations:
* Use past tense throughout, as the country no longer exists.
* Clearly state the dates of existence and the circumstances of foundation and dissolution.
* Base the article on what historians have documented. Do not speculate about the country's nature or significance beyond what sources say.
* Distinguish clearly between the historical territory and any modern country that may occupy the same geographic area.
</instructions>
