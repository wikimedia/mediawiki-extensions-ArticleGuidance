<instructions>
Articles about human settlements such as towns, villages, and hamlets are appropriate when the settlement has received significant coverage in reliable, independent sources, or is documented in official records. Very small settlements may need independent coverage beyond administrative listings to justify their relevance.
Quick considerations:
* State the settlement's location, country, and administrative status clearly in the lead.
* Use official national statistics and census data for population figures.
* Distinguish between the settlement as a geographic place and any administrative unit it belongs to.
* Do not write about a settlement solely because you live there.
</instructions>
