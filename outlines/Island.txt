<instructions>
Islands that are named, listed in authoritative databases, and historically relevant, are appropriate subjects for articles. Very small or uninhabited islands may be notable if they have received significant independent coverage or have geographic, historical, or ecological significance.
Quick considerations:
* State the island's location, size, and administrative status clearly in the lead.
* Use authoritative geographic databases and national statistics for factual data.
* Distinguish between the island as a geographic feature and any settlement or administrative unit on it.
* Do not write about an island solely because you live there or have a personal connection to it.
</instructions>
