<instructions>
Landforms (mountains, volcanoes, rivers, valleys, lakes, and other natural physical features of the Earth's surface) are generally considered notable when they are named, documented in authoritative geographic or scientific sources, and of sufficient size or significance to warrant a standalone article. Very minor or unnamed features typically do not need their own article.
Quick considerations:
* State the feature's location, country, and physical type clearly in the lead.
* Use authoritative geographic databases and national mapping agencies for factual data such as elevation, length, or area.
* Base scientific content on peer-reviewed geology, geography, or earth sciences publications.
* Do not write about a feature solely because you live near it or have a personal connection to it.
* If the feature is part of a larger system (a mountain range, river basin, or volcanic field), check whether a standalone article is needed or whether the information belongs in the parent article.
</instructions>
