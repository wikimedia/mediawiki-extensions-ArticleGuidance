<instructions>
Articles about literary works are appropriate when the work has received significant coverage in reliable, independent sources such as literary reviews, academic criticism, or established reference works. A plot summary alone is not sufficient for an article. There must be sourced external commentary on real-world topics such as themes, style, and reception.
Quick considerations:
* Never write an article that consists solely of a plot summary. Independent critical commentary is required.
* Write about the work from an outside perspective. Describe the plot in the present tense and keep the summary to less than 500 words for full-length novels.
* Do not write about a work you have authored or are affiliated with.
* Cite literary critics, scholars, and established reviewers, not reader reviews on Amazon or social media.
</instructions>
