<instructions>
Articles about medical conditions are appropriate only when the condition is recognised in mainstream medical literature. Self-diagnosed conditions, informal illness categories, and conditions described only in non-peer-reviewed or alternative medicine sources are not suitable for Wikipedia articles.
Quick considerations:
*Write from the perspective of established medical and scientific consensus. Avoid fringe theories or unproven treatments.
*Use medical terminology correctly. Link technical terms on first use.
*Do not write in the second person ("you may experience..."). Wikipedia is an encyclopaedia, not a patient information leaflet.
*Cite peer-reviewed publications, clinical guidelines, and recognised health authorities, not news articles or personal accounts.
</instructions>
