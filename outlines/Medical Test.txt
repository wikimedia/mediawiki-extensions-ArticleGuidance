<instructions>
Articles about medical tests are appropriate only when the test is recognised in mainstream medical and scientific literature. Tests described only in non-peer-reviewed sources, promotional materials, or alternative medicine publications are not suitable for Wikipedia articles.
Quick considerations:
* Write from the perspective of established medical and scientific consensus.
* Do not write in the second person. Wikipedia is a reference work, not a patient guide.
* Cite peer-reviewed publications and clinical guidelines, not health journalism or patient information leaflets.
* Distinguish clearly between what the test measures, what it is used to diagnose, and how it is performed.
</instructions>
