<instructions>
Articles about museums are appropriate when the museum has received significant coverage in reliable, independent sources. Being open to the public or holding a permanent collection does not by itself establish notability. Coverage must be substantive and come from sources that are not the museum's own promotional materials.

Quick considerations:
* State the museum's location, type, and founding date clearly in the introduction.
* Describe the collection based on what independent sources say, not solely on the museum's own promotional materials.
* Do not write about a museum you are employed by or affiliated with.
* Avoid promotional language. Do not describe the museum as 'world-class' or 'one of the finest' unless directly supported by a cited independent source.
</instructions>
