<instructions>
Articles about music genres are appropriate when the genre is recognised by music scholars, critics, or established music publications. Informal genre labels used only on social media or fan communities, and micro-genres without independent critical coverage, are not suitable for standalone Wikipedia articles.
Quick considerations:
* Define the genre clearly: what musical characteristics define it, and how it relates to other genres.
* Base the article on what music scholars, critics, and established publications have written. Do not define genres based on personal opinion.
* Do not include lists of artists. Genre articles should not serve as directories.
* Music genre names are not proper nouns and should not be capitalised unless they contain a proper name (e.g. British invasion, not British Invasion).
</instructions>
