<instructions>
Articles about musicians are appropriate when the person has received significant coverage in reliable, independent sources. Being active as a musician, having released recordings, or having performed publicly does not by itself establish notability. The person needs in-depth coverage from sources that are not connected to them or their management.
Quick considerations:
* Focus on the person's career and documented achievements, not their personal life unless it is well-sourced and significant for general reference.
* Cite established music publications and major news outlets, not the musician's own website, social media, or press releases.
* Do not list every song or performance. Focus on significant works supported by independent sources.
* Do not write about yourself, your family, or your friends.
</instructions>
