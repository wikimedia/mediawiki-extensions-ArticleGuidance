<instructions>
Articles about people are among the most frequently deleted on Wikipedia. The person needs in-depth coverage from at least two articles published by renowned publishers, two years apart.

Quick considerations:
* Start with who this person is and why they are notable. Every claim needs a reliable, independent source that covers this person in depth; brief passing mentions or a single news story are not enough.
* Write in the third person and keep a neutral tone throughout.
* Don’t write about yourself, your family, or your friends.
</instructions>
