<article-guidance article-type="Q756">

<instructions>
Any plant species that has been given a formal scientific name in a peer-reviewed publication is considered appropriate for a Wikipedia article. The article should accurately represent current scientific understanding based on peer-reviewed sources.
Quick considerations:
* Use the species' common name as the article title where one exists, and include the scientific name in the lead.
* Plant species names (common names) are not capitalised unless they contain a proper noun.
* Base all scientific claims on peer-reviewed publications and authoritative botanical references.
* Conservation status should follow the IUCN Red List where available.
</instructions>
