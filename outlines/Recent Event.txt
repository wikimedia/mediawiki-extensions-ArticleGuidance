<instructions>
Wikipedia is not a news service. Articles about recent events are only appropriate when there is already substantial coverage in multiple independent reliable sources and when the event is likely to have lasting significance. Breaking news that has not yet been covered in depth should not be turned into a Wikipedia article.
Quick considerations:
*Write in the past tense, even if the event is ongoing. Wikipedia documents events for general reference, not in real time.
*Stick to what is verifiable from reliable sources at the time of writing. Do not speculate about causes, consequences, or future developments.
*Avoid copying from news articles. Paraphrase and cite.
*If the event is still developing, consider whether it is too early to write a stable article.
</instructions>
