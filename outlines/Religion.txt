<instructions>
Articles about religions and religious traditions are appropriate when the religion is documented in reliable, independent academic sources. New religious movements, informal spiritual communities, and personal belief systems described only in self-published sources are not appropriate for standalone Wikipedia articles. Religion articles must be written from a neutral point of view. Wikipedia does not take positions on religious truth claims. The article should describe what the religion teaches and how it is practised, not evaluate whether its beliefs are correct.
Quick considerations:
* Write from a strictly neutral perspective. Do not endorse, promote, or disparage any religion or its beliefs.
* Distinguish clearly between what adherents believe and what scholarly sources have documented about the religion.
* Base all claims about history, doctrine, and practice on peer-reviewed academic sources, not on religious texts used as the sole source.
* Do not write about a religion you are a member of or have a personal interest in promoting.
</instructions>
