<instructions>
Senior secondary schools (educating students to at least age 15) are generally considered notable. Primary and middle schools are typically not notable enough for their own Wikipedia article unless they have received significant independent coverage. The article must be based on reliable, third-party sources, not the school's own website.
Quick considerations:
*Explain what makes this school notable, its history, alumni, programmes, or achievements documented in independent sources.
*Avoid promotional language: rankings claims, admissions boasts, and superlatives must be supported by cited independent sources.
*Do not write about a school you attended, work at, or are affiliated with.
*Primary and elementary schools do not normally meet notability requirements and should not have standalone articles.
</instructions>
