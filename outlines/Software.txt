<article-guidance article-type="Q7397" notability-risk="sources wikidata" category="Software">

<instructions>
Articles about software are appropriate when the software has received significant coverage in reliable, independent sources. Software being available for download, having many users, or being open source does not by itself establish notability. Coverage must be substantive and come from sources that are not connected to the software's developers.
Quick considerations:
* Write from a neutral perspective based on facts. Do not describe the software in promotional terms.
* Cite independent technology publications and academic sources, not the developer's own documentation, blog, or press releases.
* Do not write about software you have developed or are affiliated with.
* If the software is a new version of existing software, check whether a separate article is needed or whether the information should be added to an existing article.
</instructions>
