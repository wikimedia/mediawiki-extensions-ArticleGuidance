Most songs don't need their own article. They can be part of articles about the song's album or the artist. A song may be notable enough for a separate article when it has a major impact on relevant music charts, has received awards, or has been recorded independently by several notable artists or groups.

Quick considerations:
* Make sure that articles exist for the artist and the album of the song.
* Consider only songs with enough verifiable material for a substantial entry. Short articles with superficial coverage are often merged into the artist or album page.
* Don’t write about songs created by you, your family, or your friends.
</instructions>
