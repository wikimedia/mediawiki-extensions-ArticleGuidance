<instructions>
Articles about sports clubs are appropriate when the club has received significant coverage in reliable, independent sources. Participation in a local or regional league alone does not establish notability. The club needs in-depth coverage beyond routine match reports and fixture listings.
Quick considerations:
* Focus on the club's history, significant achievements, and documented impact, using independent sources.
* Avoid promotional language: do not describe the club as 'one of the biggest' or 'most successful' unless directly supported by a cited independent source.
* Do not write about a club you play for, manage, or are affiliated with.
* Club names and stadium names are proper nouns and should be capitalised.
</instructions>
