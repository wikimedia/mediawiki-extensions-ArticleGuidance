<instructions>
Articles about sportspeople are frequently deleted unless the person has received significant coverage in reliable, independent sources. Participation alone — even at a professional level — is generally not enough. The person needs in-depth coverage, not just statistics or match results in sports databases.

Quick considerations:

* Focus on what makes this person notable beyond their statistics.
* Cite independent journalism, not just databases or club websites. Results and stats alone do not establish notability — the coverage around them does.
* Don’t write about yourself, your family, or your friends.

</instructions>

<recommended-sources>
  <info>Established sports journalism outlets with editorial standards (e.g. ESPN, BBC Sport, L'Équipe), </info>
  <info>Official league, federation or governing body records, Peer-reviewed sports science or biographical publications</info>
  <info>Major newswires covering sport (e.g. Reuters, AP)</info>
</recommended-sources>

<discouraged-sources>
  <info>Sports statistics databases used as the sole source (e.g. Transfermarkt, Soccerway, Basketball-Reference) 	</info>
  <info>Fan sites, supporter blogs and club forums</info>
  <info>Social media accounts including the athlete's own profiles</info>
  <info>Self-published sources including athlete websites and press releases</info>
</discouraged-sources>

</article-guidance>
