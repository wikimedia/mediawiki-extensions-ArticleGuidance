<article-guidance article-type="Q5398426" notability-risk="sources junior" category="Television series">

<instructions>
Articles about television series are appropriate when the series has received significant coverage in reliable, independent sources beyond routine listings and press releases. A series being broadcast does not automatically make it notable. Coverage must be substantive and independent.
Quick considerations:
* Write from an outside perspective, not as if you are describing the show to a fan. Focus on real-world context: who made it, when it aired, how it was received.
* Avoid plot-heavy writing. The plot section should be a brief summary, not a detailed recap.
* Do not write about a series you are involved in or have a personal connection to.
</instructions>
