<instructions>
Wikipedia covers theorems that have been formally proved and are recognised in peer-reviewed mathematical literature. Unproved conjectures, informal mathematical claims, and results published only in non-peer-reviewed sources are not appropriate for standalone articles.
Quick considerations:
*State the theorem precisely. Use standard mathematical notation where appropriate.
*Explain why the theorem matters, what it proves and where it is used.
*Do not include original proofs or new mathematical content. Wikipedia is not a research platform.
*Long or highly technical proofs are typically summarised or omitted. If a proof is included, it should be short and illuminating.
</instructions>
