<instructions>
Universities and degree-awarding institutions are generally considered notable. However, the article must be based on reliable, independent sources, not the institution's own website, prospectus, or promotional materials. Avoid academic boosterism: rankings, admissions statistics, and awards should only be included if supported by independent sources.
Quick considerations:
*Focus on the institution's history, academic character, and achievements based on independent sources.
*Avoid promotional language: do not describe the university as "prestigious", "world-class", or "leading" unless directly quoted from a cited independent source.
*Do not list every degree programme, department, or student club, focus on what is notable and well-sourced.
*Do not write about a university you attend, work at, or are affiliated with.
</instructions>
