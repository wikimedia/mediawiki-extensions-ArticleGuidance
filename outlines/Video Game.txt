<instructions>
Articles about video games are appropriate when the game has received significant coverage in reliable, independent sources. Being available for download, having many players, or being listed in a store does not by itself establish notability. Coverage must be substantive and come from sources that are not the game's publisher or developer.
Quick considerations:
* Write from a neutral, encyclopedic perspective. Do not describe the game in promotional terms.
* Cite independent game publications and reviews, not the publisher's own press releases or official game website.
* Do not write about a game you have developed or are affiliated with.
* If the game is part of a series, check whether a standalone article is needed or whether the information belongs in the series article.
* Avoid in-universe writing. Describe the game's mechanics and plot from an outside perspective.
* If another article exists for the game title, adjust the title to include the year in parentheses, for example: "[Game title] (2023 video game)".
</instructions>
