<article-guidance article-type="*" notability-risk="sources">

<instructions>
Wikipedia covers subjects that have received significant coverage in reliable sources that are independent of the subject. Before you write, check that such coverage exists. If it does not, the article is likely to be deleted however well it is written.

Quick considerations:

* Summarise what independent sources say. Do not add your own analysis, opinion or conclusions.
* Open with a short lead that says what the subject is and why it is notable.
* Cite a source for every claim that a reader could reasonably question.
* Write in a neutral tone. Avoid promotional language and loaded words.
* Do not write about yourself, your employer, your family or your friends.
* Do not copy text from other websites or books. Write it in your own words.
</instructions>

  <info>Official records from governments and regulators, for factual details</info>
</recommended-sources>

<discouraged-sources>

  <info>Other Wikipedia articles and user-generated wikis</info>
  <info>Directory listings and aggregator databases used as the sole source</info>
</discouraged-sources>
